MGL E&C awarded Central Thermal Power Plant IV contract

12 June 20254 min readShare

The firm will lead structural and mechanical delivery on the largest energy project commissioned in the region this decade.

The Ministry of Energy has awarded MGL E&C the structural and mechanical delivery package for Central Thermal Power Plant IV, a 450 MW facility serving Ulaanbaatar and the surrounding aimags.

Scope of the award

The package covers the turbine hall, boiler house and stack, together with the full mechanical installation across the generation block. Work begins with ground investigation this quarter.

Commissioning is fixed to the national winter heating schedule, which sets a hard programme constraint rather than a target date. Sequencing has been planned to absorb weather and supply risk without moving handover.

What happens next

  • Ground investigation and geotechnical modelling — Q3
  • Foundation design sign-off — Q4
  • Main structural works commence — Q1 following
