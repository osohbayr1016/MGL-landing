Overview

Central Thermal Power Plant IV is the largest generation project commissioned in the region this decade. MGL E&C led structural and mechanical delivery across a 38-month programme, working to a fixed commissioning date tied to the national winter heating schedule.

The site sits on seasonally unstable ground, so the foundation strategy was resolved first: a piled raft tied into bedrock, designed to tolerate the freeze-thaw cycle without differential settlement under turbine loading.

Scope

  • Structural design and analysis of the turbine hall, boiler house and stack
  • Geotechnical investigation and permafrost-aware foundation design
  • Mechanical installation and pipework across the generation block
  • Construction management, programme and cost control
  • Testing, commissioning and operational handover
